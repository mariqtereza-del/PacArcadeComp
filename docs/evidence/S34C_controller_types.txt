S34C: controller types, icon and button pictures (script part; the user's test with real pads is still to come)

Built: PadType (Xbox, PS4, PS5, other) from SDL_GameControllerGetType (360/One = Xbox, PS3/PS4 = PS4, PS5 = PS5, anything else or a non-standard pad = other); a controller icon (16x10, drawn in code) in the menu header, the Bindings screen header ("PS5 PAD") and the key-help panel, plus the type word in the menu footer; a 3.5 s notice "PS4 CONNECTED" / "PS4 REMOVED" with the icon when a pad is plugged in or removed (also at start-up); button pictures drawn in code (no image files): Xbox A green, B red, X blue, Y yellow discs; PlayStation cross, circle, square, triangle shapes in their colours; labelled boxes for LB/RB or L1/R1, LT/RT or L2/R2, VIEW/MENU or SHARE/CREATE/OPTIONS, L3, R3, SHARE (Xbox) / MIC (PS5), TOUCH, P1-P4; unknown pads keep the plain names. Used in the Bindings pad column and the key-help panel (pad buttons after each key).
Files: src/app/menu.* (all drawing), src/app/main.cpp (wiring: type, notice, hot-plug). No change to emulated input bits.

Tests (tests/test_padtypes.sh, real window on Xvfb with pretend pads of each type, driven by simulated pad buttons, screenshots analysed for the picture colours):
- Xbox 360 and Xbox One: icon word XBOX; A, B, X, Y discs found in all four colours, no PlayStation shapes.
- PS4 and PS5: icon words PS4 / PS5; cross, circle, square, triangle found in their colours, no Xbox discs.
- Other (generic pad): icon PAD, plain names, no pictures.
- All five: the "connected" notice is on the screen.
Screenshots: docs/evidence/S34C_bindings_xbox.png, S34C_bindings_ps5.png (from build/s34c).
Trigger and touch-pad pictures: L2/LT, R2/RT boxes are in the screenshots; TOUCH and SHARE/MIC are covered by the label table (tests/test_menu.cpp).
Regression: scripted hashes unchanged (2400 ticks at 180 Hz 60c4b12eb084ed7e, 3000 ticks d7d39438e9e8d70c); test_menu, test_bindings, test_controls, test_state, test_purist (7,273 hashes = prototype), test_replay, test_padbuttons, test_rumble, test_haptics, run_all: see the end of this file.
