T02f evidence: mode timers and level settings in use; all pieces together; full regression (2026-10-16)
Readable code: src/logic/mode_timers.h; adapters in src/rules/rules.h (namespace timers); the instruction model is src/rules/z80sim.h.
ROM routines replaced when "--rules readable" is on: 0E36 (scatter / chase timer, every frame), 1376 (frightened timer, every frame), 070E (level start: the speed patterns, phase limits, ghost house limits, Cruise Elroy limits, frightened and inactivity times). The level start's last part, the drawing of the level's fruits (ROM 2BEA), stays ROM code: the hook does everything up to the CALL and carries on in the ROM there.
With T02a-T02f 19 ROM routines are replaced by their readable versions (random numbers, scoring and extra life, dots / energizers / ghost house counters / fruit, ghost targeting and direction chooser, movement of Pac-Man and the four ghosts, the timers and the level start). Purist and the normal game still use only the original ROM routines.
