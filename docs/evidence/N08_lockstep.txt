N08: Reusable lockstep harness  (2026-10-16)
Tools: tests/lockstep.h (the harness), tests/test_lockstep.sh (in tests/run_all.sh), tests/test_rules_random.cpp/.sh (the T02a test, now a user of the harness).
What the harness compares after EVERY frame: work RAM (stack included), video RAM, colour RAM, sprite RAM and coordinates, sound registers, latches, interrupt vector, watchdog count, the I/O writes of the frame, the cycle count of the frame, and the CPU registers (first difference reported). Garbage mode fills the registers an adapter leaves alone. Coverage players drive it: inputs and RAM pokes are copied to the variants, and the variants start afresh whenever the player starts a new machine.
