X41 glow fix evidence (2026-10-14/15)
User report: with the curve on, the glow 'reflections' of moving elements were too spaced apart and too visible (screenshot).
Cause: curved-mesh drawing used the picture textures' texture coordinates (0 to 0.64) for the picture-sized glow texture (0 to 1): glow stretched about 1.57x. Flat glow was correct.
Fix: draw_mesh(texture, own_size) in src/app/main.cpp; glow gain 0.3 Light / 0.6 Strong (was 0.5 / 1.0).
