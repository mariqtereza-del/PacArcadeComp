What it plays: 15 games in turn with different DIP settings (3, 1, 2 and 5 lives; bonus life at 10000, 15000, 20000 and none; normal and hard; both ghost-name modes; free play, 1 coin/1 credit, 1 coin/2 credits, 2 coins/1 credit), one and two players, start levels 1 to 17 (so every fruit). In every round: the top-left power pill is eaten and the four ghosts are met one after the other; the dot counter is put at 69 so the fruit comes and Pac-Man is put three tiles from it and walks onto it; the score is put just below the bonus score so the extra life is earned; rounds are cleared with the dot-counter trick. After 9000 frames of a game the scripted help stops so every game ends. Pokes exist only in tests.
Lessons recorded: (1) the ROM reads the DIP switches only at power-on, so every game with other switches starts on a freshly powered-on machine; (2) in free play the credit state is shown from the start, so the player must not wait for attract mode only; (3) Pac-Man must be put on the tile beside the power pill once, not on every frame, or he never walks onto it.
