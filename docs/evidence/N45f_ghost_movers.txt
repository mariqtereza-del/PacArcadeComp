Scope notes (honest): (1) Names come from reading the code, not from the project's label list. (2) The four entries 1B36, 1C4B, 1D22, 1DF9 already had a readable-rules hook from step T02e (the model in src/logic/movement.h) that runs the whole routine in almost every call; the ROM addresses still executed were the rare cases where that hook hands over to the ROM (stray state, end of the frame inside the routine). This step makes the hand-over go to the native instruction runner instead (one line in ghost_move in src/rules/rules.h) and installs the runner at the entries and at the points inside the routines where the ROM continues. (3) The test therefore checks the runner itself at all 19 entries against the ROM (random states and real states); in the lockstep runs the four main entries are compared one to one with the ROM; the entries inside the routines are only reached after a hand-over, so there the check is that they ran and not more often than the ROM entered them. (4) The progress meter line printed by the test ("before 1257") removes the older T02e hooks at the four entries for its "before" figure; the true figure before this step is 1,197 (end of N45e), after 1,064 (measured with the same meter without removing them). (5) Only the readable-rules mode uses the native code; Purist mode and original-rules mode always run the ROM. (6) One new instruction kind: ADC HL,HL. (7) The full run_all.sh was NOT run (not a milestone; next one is N45g).
