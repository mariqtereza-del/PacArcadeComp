# STEP X04: Mouse in the menus: hover and click
Status: **VERIFIED**   Lane: EXTRAS   Depends on: S34V

OBJECTIVE: With the menu open the mouse pointer appears; moving over an item selects it, left click chooses it, right click goes back. The pointer is hidden during play.

WHY: Every modern game menu works with a mouse.

STARTING STATE: The steps it depends on are VERIFIED. Rules shared by every X step are at the top of tools/plan_data_x.py (assist rule, Purist off, new code in new files).

READ FIRST: docs/notes/ram_map.md, PROJECT_STATUS.md and the files named in MAY TOUCH

MAY TOUCH (only these): src/app/menu.*, src/app/main.cpp (wiring)

IMPLEMENT:
- Map window pixels to menu items (the menu is drawn at 224 x 288 in the picture, scaled by the window).
-  Hover selects; click = Enter; right click = Esc; left/right half of a choice item steps it down/up.
- Pointer hidden again when the menu closes; Purist keeps the mouse menu (it is the pause menu, not an arcade addition).

DO NOT:
- No change to the emulation: the extra only reads memory, lives outside the machine, or is an assist (the game then stops counting for records).
- The Reset to defaults button puts it back to its default unless this step says otherwise; replay hashes must stay identical (tests/test_purist.sh, tests/test_replay.sh).

VERIFY:
- Unit test: pixel to item mapping at several window sizes and scalings
- Real-window test: xdotool moves and clicks drive the menu to Controls and back
- Scripted replay and Purist hashes unchanged

SUCCESS CRITERIA: All checks pass; replay and Purist hashes identical.

IF FAILURE: Stop. Re-run only this step's VERIFY command. Undo the last edit and retry. Compare against the Python prototype (tools/analysis) as the reference. Write what you found in docs/evidence/X04_failure.md. Do not start the next step.

- Esc to open the menu, move the mouse over the items (the highlight follows), click Display, click Back, right-click to close.
