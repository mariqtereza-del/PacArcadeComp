# STEP X09: Lightbar: yellow while you play
Status: **VERIFIED**   Lane: EXTRAS   Depends on: S34V

OBJECTIVE: Pads with a lightbar (DualShock 4, DualSense) glow Pac-Man yellow during play, dim blue in the menu, and go back to the pad's normal colour on exit. Setting: Lightbar On/Off.

WHY: First, smallest part of the lightbar: prove the pad accepts colours and that they are put back.

STARTING STATE: The steps it depends on are VERIFIED. Rules shared by every X step are at the top of tools/plan_data_x.py (assist rule, Purist off, new code in new files).

READ FIRST: docs/notes/ram_map.md, PROJECT_STATUS.md and the files named in MAY TOUCH

MAY TOUCH (only these): src/app/lightbar.h (new), src/app/menu.*, src/app/main.cpp (wiring)

IMPLEMENT:
- SDL_GameControllerHasLED / SDL_GameControllerSetLED; remember nothing to restore (SDL returns control on close; set to a neutral blue).
-  Xbox pads and DS4Windows pads have no lightbar: nothing happens, nothing breaks.
- Setting in settings.cfg, Controls page; Purist off.

DO NOT:
- No change to the emulation: the extra only reads memory, lives outside the machine, or is an assist (the game then stops counting for records).
- The Reset to defaults button puts it back to its default unless this step says otherwise; replay hashes must stay identical (tests/test_purist.sh, tests/test_replay.sh).

VERIFY:
- Pretend DS4 and DualSense pads (SDL virtual joystick with a SetLED callback) receive the colours
- Xbox pretend pad receives nothing
- Scripted replay and Purist hashes unchanged

SUCCESS CRITERIA: All checks pass; replay and Purist hashes identical.
