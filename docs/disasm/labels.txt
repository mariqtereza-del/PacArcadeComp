# S09 labels. One line per routine: ADDRESS | name | confidence | kind | purpose | evidence (code reading) 
# Names are descriptive working names chosen by us (the ROM has no names). Confidence: HIGH = the code does exactly this and the prototype run
# confirms it is reached; MED = clear from the code, role inferred; LOW = guess from a few instructions. First-reached frames are measured by
# tools/analysis/oracle_s09.py and stored in docs/evidence/S09_prototype_trace.txt (tests/check_labels.py compares them).
0000 | reset_entry | HIGH | boot | Power-on/reset entry: disable interrupts, set interrupt page register I=3F, jump to boot_init. | DI; LD A,3F; LD I,A; JP 230B at 0000-0007. Also the target of the service-switch restart at 01D1.
0008 | fill_bytes | HIGH | helper (RST 08) | Write A to B consecutive bytes starting at HL (HL ends after the block). | LD (HL),A; INC HL; DJNZ; RET at 0008-000C. Called with RST 08 from boot_init/main_init (e.g. 2358, 235E).
0010 | read_table_byte | HIGH | helper (RST 10) | HL = HL + A, then A = byte at HL. | ADD A,L; LD L,A; LD A,0; ADC A,H; LD H,A; LD A,(HL); RET at 0010-0017.
0018 | read_table_word | HIGH | helper (RST 18) | HL = word at HL + 2*B (a table of 16-bit values). | LD A,B; ADD A,A; RST 10; LD E,A; INC HL; LD D,(HL); EX DE,HL; RET at 0018-001F.
0020 | jump_table_dispatch | HIGH | helper (RST 20) | Jump through a table of 16-bit addresses that follows the RST; A is the index (A*2 added to the table address). Never returns to the caller: the table handler returns to whatever the caller pushed. | POP HL; ADD A,A; RST 10; LD E,A; INC HL; LD D,(HL); EX DE,HL; JP (HL) at 0020-0027. 21 tables found in S08.
0028 | queue_task_inline | HIGH | helper (RST 28) | Put a 2-byte task (id, parameter) that follows the RST into the task queue, then continue after those two bytes. | POP HL; LD B,(HL); INC HL; LD C,(HL); INC HL; PUSH HL; JR 0042 at 0028-002E; 75 call sites with two inline bytes.
0030 | schedule_timed_event_inline | HIGH | helper (RST 30) | Find a free 3-byte slot in the 16-slot table at 4C90 (first byte zero) and copy the 3 inline bytes after the RST into it. | LD DE,4C90; LD B,10; JP 0051; 0051-0064 scans slots in steps of 3, copies 3 bytes from the return address; 23 call sites.
0038 | rst38_lockup | MED | helper (RST 38) | Endless loop that writes 0 to 5000 (interrupts off) and 5007 and never kicks the watchdog, so the machine resets. | XOR A; LD (5000),A; LD (5007),A; JP 0038 at 0038-0041. Not expected to run (RST 38 = byte FF executed by mistake).
0042 | task_queue_push | HIGH | helper | Store B then C at the queue write pointer (word at 4C80) and advance it, wrapping the low byte back to C0 (queue lives at 4CC0-4CFF). | LD HL,(4C80); LD (HL),B; INC L; LD (HL),C; INC L; JR NZ; LD L,C0; LD (4C80),HL at 0042-0050. Reader: task_loop at 238D uses 4C82.
008D | vblank_interrupt_handler | HIGH | interrupt | Per-frame interrupt: kick watchdog, interrupts off, copy sound registers and sprites to the hardware, run the per-frame task calls (see boot_flow.md), re-enable interrupts and return. | Word at 3FFC = 008D and the program sets the vector byte to FC (OUT (0),A); prototype shows the vector byte FA at boot and FC later.
01DC | update_frame_counters | LOW | interrupt step | Increments/decrements three RAM counters at 4C84-4C86 and cascades them against the table at 0219, then updates 4C8A-4C8C. | 01DC-0218. Meaning of the counters not decided (S10).
0221 | run_timed_events | MED | interrupt step | Walk the 16 three-byte slots at 4C90: count each slot down, and when its count reaches zero dispatch through the 10-entry table at 0247 using the slot's two data bytes. | 0221-0262: compares top 2 bits against 4C8A, DEC (HL), tests low 6 bits for zero, pushes return address 025B and RST 20.
03C8 | game_mode_dispatch | MED | interrupt step | Dispatch on the byte at 4E00 through the 4-entry table at 03CC (entries 03D4, 03FE, 05E5, 06BE); entry 03D4 further dispatches on 4E01. | 03C8-03D7.
0267 | read_coin_inputs | MED | interrupt step | Reads IN0 at 5000: three bits (coin 1, coin 2, service coin, active low) are each shifted through a counter at 4E66-4E68; when a counter reaches 0C a coin is counted in BCD at 4E6E (capped at 99). | 0267-02AC reads 5000, uses RLC/RLA/AND 0F/SUB 0C, DAA, cap 99. Bit meanings from docs/notes/hardware_facts.md section 6.
02AD | coin_counter_pulse | LOW | interrupt step | Drives output 5007 high then low over 16 calls for each pending coin counted at 4E69. | 02AD-02DE: LD (5007),A with 1 then 0.
02FD | update_player_labels | HIGH | interrupt step | Writes three tile codes into the top row of the screen at 43D8 (player 1) / 43C5 (player 2), blinking on bit 4 of the counter at 4DCE; also drives latch outputs 5004/5005. | 02FD-0398: tile codes 50,55,31 / 50,55,32 / 40 40 40 (blank). Prototype: these exact bytes are written to 43D8-43DA and 43C5-43C7 by 036D-0382 in frames 301-302, and the screen text dump shows 1UP and 2UP at frame 302; the blink is read from the code (bit 4 of 4DCE), not yet watched.
2D0C | sound_update_group_A | MED | interrupt step | Updates three sound channels whose state is at 4E9C/4EAC/4EBC using tables at 3B30/3B40/3B80 and writes results to the register shadow at 4E8C-4E9B. | 2D0C-2D43: LD IX,4E9C; LD IY,4E8C; tables 3B30,3B40,3B80.
2CC1 | sound_update_group_B | MED | interrupt step | Same for the three channels at 4ECC/4EDC/4EEC (tables 3BC8,3BCC,3BD0), writing to 4E8C-4E9B. In the handler, when this group's flag byte is nonzero its waveform (4ECF/4EDF/4EEF) is used instead of group A's. | 2CC1-2D0B; handler lines 00A8-00D2 choose between 4ECF and 4E9F, etc.
230B | boot_init | HIGH | boot | First initialisation: zero the 8 latch bytes at 5000-5007, fill video RAM with tile 40 (blank) and colour RAM with 0F, set interrupt mode 2, vector byte FA, enable interrupts (5000=1), then HALT until the first interrupt. | 230B-234A: LD (50C0),A watchdog kicks; IM 2; LD A,FA; OUT (0),A; LD A,1; LD (5000),A; EI; HALT.
234B | main_init | HIGH | boot | Reached after the self-test: set stack to 4FC0, clear latches, RAM 4C00-4EF7 and 5040-507F, clear colour and video RAM through the helpers, set the task queue pointers (4C80 and 4C82) to 4CC0, fill the queue with FF, enable interrupts and fall into task_loop. | 234B-238C.
238D | task_loop | HIGH | boot | Main loop: wait until the queue entry at pointer 4C82 is a task (top bit clear), mark it consumed (FF), read its parameter, advance the pointer (wrap to C0) and run the task through the 32-entry table at 23A8; each task returns to 238D. | 238D-23A7.
23A8 | task_table | HIGH | data | 32 task entry points (word table used by task_loop). | RST 20 table at 23A8 (S08 found 32 entries).
23ED | clear_video_ram | MED | boot helper | Fill video RAM with tile 40 (blank): B=0 all four pages 4000-43FF, B=1 only 4040 onward for 0x200 bytes. | 23ED-240C.
240D | clear_colour_ram | HIGH | boot helper | Fill colour RAM 4400-47FF with 00. | 240D-2418: A=0; HL=4400; 4 times RST 08 with B=0.
2052 | colour_address_of_tile | LOW | helper | Calls 0065 for the video RAM address and adds 0400 to give the matching colour RAM address. | 2052-2059.
0065 | screen_pos_to_video_address | MED | helper | Converts a screen position (L and H, each offset by 20) into a video RAM address: 4040 + (H-20)*32 + (L-20). | 0065 jumps to 202D; 202D-2051 does the arithmetic (five left shifts of H with carry into B, add L, add 4040).
3000 | power_on_self_test | MED | boot | The routine the first interrupt (vector byte FA, word at 3FFA = 3000) runs: ROM checksum loop, RAM test, then a test-mode screen that reads inputs and DIP switches; leaving it with the service switch ends in a jump to main_init at 234B. | 3000-32EA: sums ROM with LD C/ADD A,(HL), tests RAM, reads 5000/5040/5080, ends at 32EA JP 234B. Prototype: first reached in frame 1 (the first interrupt is delivered at the end of frame 0); runs until frame 253, then control passes to 234B at frame 296.
# ==== S11 gameplay labels (read by tests/check_gameplay.py; the S09 tools stop reading at the line above) ====
# Same format. The step allows at most 8 routines, so only the eight below are named here; other routines found on the way are listed in docs/notes/gameplay_routines.md.
2966 | ghost_pick_direction_toward_target | HIGH | gameplay | Ghost target choice, the core: given the ghost's tile (HL), a target tile (DE) and its current direction (A), tries the four directions in order right, down, left, up, never reverses, skips wall tiles (tile code & C0 = C0), measures the squared distance from the next tile to the target (29EA with the 8-bit multiply at 2A12) and returns the direction and vector with the smallest distance. The four per-ghost target tasks (task 08 at 2730 Blinky, 09 at 276C Pinky, 0A at 27A9 Inky, 0B at 27F1 Clyde) only decide which target to pass in. | S11 trace section 10: 559 calls in a scripted game from six call sites (the four target tasks); never reversed; Blinky chase calls (return address 2765) pass exactly Pac-Man's tile (4D39/4D3A) 82 of 82 times; Blinky scatter calls (2751) pass 221D every time. Code read at 2966-29E9, 2730-27A8.
1B36 | ghost_blinky_move | HIGH | gameplay | One frame of Blinky's movement: tunnel check (205A sets 4D99), speed-pattern shift (4D5A/4D5C), and when the pattern says move: at a tile centre (pixel & 7 = 4) queue the direction-choice task 08 (or 0C when frightened) via RST 28h, apply the pending reversal (1EFE), add the vector (4D14) to the pixel position 4D00/01 (1C36-1C44) and refresh the tile 4D31 (2018). Pinky, Inky and Clyde have copies at 1C4B, 1D22 and 1DF9. | S11 trace sections 1 and write trace: 4D00/4D01 written only at pc 1C44 (and boot/reset code); executed 5012 times in scenario A, once per gameplay_frame_update call except while Pac-Man dies; position bytes feed the sprite staging checked in S10.
0E36 | ghost_mode_timer | HIGH | gameplay | The scatter/chase schedule: unless a power pill is active (4DA6), counts frames in the word 4DC2/3 and compares it with the entry 4DC1 of the table at 4D86 (frames at 60 per second). When equal it advances the phase index 4DC1 (even = scatter, odd = chase, tested at 2733 and 276F) and sets the reversal flags 4DB1-4DB4 so every ghost turns round (1EFE). | S11 trace section 3: 4DC1 went 0 to 1 at frame 1954 when the counter was 419 (table word 420 = 7 s); the table holds 420, 1620, 2040, 3240, 3540 frames = 7, 27, 34, 54, 59 s; 4DC1 returned to 0 at each life lost.
19E0 | eat_dot_at_pacman_tile | HIGH | gameplay | Dot eating: looks at the tile under Pac-Man (4D39); if it is a dot (10) or energizer (14) it adds 1 to the eaten count 4E0E, blanks the tile (40), schedules the score by queueing task 19 (add_score) with index 0 for a dot or 1 for an energizer (10 and 50 points in the table at 2B17), sets Pac-Man's pause 4D9D (1 frame for a dot, 6 for an energizer), counts the dot for ghost-house release (1B08), starts the power pill for an energizer (1A6A to 1A70), and alternates the two dot sound bits in 4EBC. | S11 trace section 4: executed 101 times in scenario A and 4E0E rose by exactly 1 in 101 frames; the only code that writes 4E0E is pc 19E7 inside it; tile code 10 appeared as the dots in S10.
0EAD | fruit_spawn_check | HIGH | gameplay | Every update: unless Pac-Man is dying (4DA5) or a fruit is already out (4DD4), when the eaten count 4E0E is 70 (46) or 170 (AA) and that fruit has not appeared yet (4E0C, 4E0D) it sets the flag, puts the fruit at position word 4DD2/3 = 8094 and chooses the fruit sprite code and colour (and a points index) for the level from a three-byte-per-level table at 0EFD (level capped at 20). | S11 trace section 8: scenario B first reached it at frame 2144 when 4E0E = 70; 4DD2 became 0x94 with sprite code 00 and colour 14 (cherry on level 1); executed once.
2A5A | add_score | HIGH | gameplay | Adds a points value to the current player's score (BCD, three bytes): not during attract (4E00 = 1); the index selects a BCD word in the points table at 2B17 (10, 50, 200, 400, 800, 1600, 100, 300, 500, 700), 2B0B finds the current player's score (4E80 or 4E84), the extra-life routine 2B33 (adds a life to 4E14/4E15 once per game and sets the sound request bit 4E9C bit 0) is called when the score passes the 4E71 limit, then the high score 4E88 is replaced if beaten and the digits are redrawn (2AAF). | S11 trace section 6: 101 calls in scenario A, score bytes changed 101 times (0 to 10 to 20 to 30 ... in BCD); S10: 4E80/4E88 match the screen digits.
2069 | ghost_house_release_check_pinky | MED | gameplay | Decides when Pinky leaves the ghost house: if 4DA1 is already set do nothing; if no life has been lost on this level (4E12 = 0) release when Pinky's personal dot counter 4E0F reaches the limit in 4DB8; after a life was lost use the global counter 4D9F (release at 7). Setting 4DA1 = 2 is done by 2086. Inky (208C, counter 4E10, limit 4DB9, global 11) and Clyde (20AF, counter 4E11, limit 4DBA, global 20) have copies. | S11 trace section 7: in scenario A Pinky was released at frame 676 with limit 0, Inky at frame 1092 with counter 4E10 = 30 and limit 30; Clyde at frame 2302 with counter 26 against limit 50 (so Clyde also leaves by some other rule, not yet found: MED). Counters are advanced by 1B08, called from the dot routine (101 calls = 101 dots).
0E6C | siren_sound_select | MED | gameplay | Sound request: sets one of the bits 0-4 in the sound request byte 4EAC according to how many dots have been eaten (thresholds 74, B4, D4, E4 in 4E0E), which makes the sound routine (S09 sound_update_group_A) play a faster siren as dots run out; clears 4EAC when Pac-Man is dying (4DA5 non-zero). | S11 trace section 9: executed every update frame (3168 times); the write trace shows 4EAC written from pcs 0E76 and 0EAC (inside this routine) among a few others (01B6 handler clear, 1AC6 power pill). The sound-side meaning of the bits is left for the audio steps.
