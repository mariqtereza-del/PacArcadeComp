## Partially complete (Python prototype, tools/analysis, analysis only)
- Boots the program ROM on the third-party `z80` 1.2.0 package (MIT) with a minimal board model; VBLANK and IM2 vector handled by hand; 50688 cycles per frame.
- Reaches attract mode; rendered frames show correct text, ghosts, colours and the demo maze (docs/evidence/prototype). Visual evidence only. Golden hashes now guard it (tests/test_prototype.py).
- Palette, tile, sprite decoders and tilemap addressing exist in render.py and look right on the screens seen.

- Called Phase 3 (study) one unit; it held five deliverables (read two sources, full disassembly, routine naming, tables, notes).
- Treated reading sources as easy; in fact the shell cannot reach the sites, the fetch tool stops at 100,000 characters of the 132,739-character Dossier and refuses to reproduce tables, and no annotated disassembly was found.
- Did not mention the prototype, which already covers part of old Phases 4 to 6 as an analysis tool.
- Planned to write a Z80 core; the proven one under the prototype is MIT-licensed C++ and can be reused.
